# Product Strategy

Synthesized from all reference material (12 PDFs) and the founding planning
session (2026-06-27). This is the settled product direction — not a proposal.

## What Houses of Thought is

An AI-powered structured reasoning platform built on John Trapasso's House of
Reason model (derived from the Paul–Elder framework for critical thinking). Users
build "houses" — layered reasoning structures that move from a question through
perspectives, evidence, assumptions, and inference to a defensible conclusion.

The AI acts as a **Socratic co-reasoning partner** (the "Collab" builder): it asks
sharpening questions, surfaces perspectives, finds and cites real sources (Research
Mode), and stress-tests logic. It does **not** write conclusions or hand out
verdicts. In student/classroom mode, the AI assistant steps back entirely.

## Positioning

**Line:** *Build the reasoning, not just the answer.*

**Problem:** People reach conclusions without structure — jumping to answers,
ignoring perspectives, leaning on unexamined assumptions. Chatbots worsen this by
delivering polished verdicts that hide the reasoning.

**Differentiator:** A chatbot gives you an answer; Houses of Thought helps you
build — and defend — the thinking behind it. The output is a transparent, traceable
house with a strength score, not a paragraph.

**Why create an account:** Save houses across devices, build a persistent Personal
Foundational POV, join or run a classroom, publish/export, track strength over
time. Try-it-now is local-only and ephemeral.

## Target audience

**Primary: Educators (teacher-led), students as end-users.**

Reasoning:
- The product's unique defensible value — AI that scaffolds but refuses to write
  the conclusion — is precisely what schools want and chatbots can't offer.
- Distribution: one teacher → a class → colleagues. Low CAC, viral.
- The Trapasso/Paul–Elder lineage is credibility educators care about.
- "Make critical thinking visible and gradeable" is a concrete, painful job.

**Secondary (supported, not the headline):**
- Individual learners and decision-makers — served by the free no-login builder;
  top-of-funnel and how teachers evaluate before adopting.
- Debate / forensics students — high-affinity niche.
- Solo founders / small-business owners — served by **Founder Mode**
  (decision 021), an opt-in, off-by-default toggle that reframes the
  builder and reasoning pipeline around business concerns and adds
  Projects with accumulating context, document upload, and retrieval.
  Extends this secondary tier; does not change the schools-first wedge above.
- Professionals/analysts (outside Founder Mode) — future expansion, not now.

## Conversion model

Free to start; no hard paywall pre-login. The front door is a **no-login builder**
(`/try`) that persists work to localStorage. Account creation carries the local
house into the new account. Monetization (likely educator/classroom seats) is TBD.

## Brand

Canonical name: **Houses of Thought** — the product and Trapasso's model share
this one name. "House of Reason" is not a real term: it was a hallucination
from an earlier migration tool (Lovable) that made it into early docs and was
mistakenly treated as fact by later sessions (decisions/001 §10). If you see
it anywhere, it's wrong. Tagline: "INTELLECTUAL BLUEPRINT · EST. 2026".
